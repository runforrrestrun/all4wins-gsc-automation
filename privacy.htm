Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Purpose

All4Wins GSC Automation is a private application used to retrieve and organize Google Search Console performance information in Google Sheets.

2. Information accessed

The application accesses Search Console data for websites that the authorized Google account can access. This includes search queries, page URLs, clicks, impressions, click-through rates, and average positions.

3. How information is used

The information is used for SEO performance monitoring, historical reporting, and monthly comparisons.

4. Data storage

Retrieved performance information is stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet controlled by the application's owner.

5. Data sharing

The application does not sell Search Console data or intentionally transmit it to third-party analytics or advertising services. Google services process data as necessary to provide the authorized functionality.

6. Access and security

Access to the application is limited to authorized Google accounts. Google OAuth is used for authorization, and the application does not collect or store Google account passwords.

7. Data retention and deletion

Imported information remains in the owner's spreadsheet until it is manually deleted. The owner can remove stored data by deleting the spreadsheet or its contents. Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
